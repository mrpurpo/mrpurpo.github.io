### Mathematics & Research

**[Optimal Move Bounds for the $2n$-Rook Exchange Problem](/papers/rook-optimality.pdf)** (2026) — Exact minimum move bounds for exchanging two opposite rows of $n$ rooks on an $m \times n$ chessboard ($m \ge 3, n \ge 2$), proving that the optimum is $4n + 1$ for $m \ge 5$ (33 moves on the standard $8 \times 8$ board) and $4n + 2$ for $m \in \{3, 4\}$. Also resolves the $8 \times 8$ exchange problem for all remaining chess pieces—kings (112), queens (23), bishops (30), knights (64), and pawns (impossible)—backed by exhaustive computational verification ([code](https://github.com/mrpurpo/16rooks), [blog write-up](/blog/solving-the-rook-exchange/)).
